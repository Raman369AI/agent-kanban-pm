# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate in all project spaces.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, and publishing another
person's private information are not acceptable. Focus criticism on ideas and
code, assume good faith where reasonable, and make room for people with
different levels of experience.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to
the repository owner through GitHub.
